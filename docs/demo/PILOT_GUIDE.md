# Guide for a future invited review

2026-10-11. Prepared for a **local, supervised synthetic usability review**. No participants have been recruited, contacted or tested. This does not start a real-data pilot. [Instructions](TESTER_INSTRUCTIONS.md), [feedback template](FEEDBACK_FORM.md), [release decision](RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md).

## Responsibility and readiness

An invited reviewer can technically use the local mock on the organiser's computer. Before a real participant session, the project owner must name the organiser, explain the exercise and agree the purpose/access/retention of minimal notes. Confirm adult-only participation and the ability to stop. The operator is a proposed role until accepted. Actual private data/notes require a separate approval; AI spending, uploads, publication and remote hosting have separate closed gates. Technical readiness is not legal/privacy pilot approval.

## Proposed protocol

Plan 15–20 minutes, not an asserted measured completion time. Explain fictional data, then assign D12-01, one of D12-02/03 and one boundary case D12-04/05/06/07. Give the task rather than the answer. Record content assistance separately. All inputs must come from the supplied profiles; no transcripts, private applicants or uploads.

Before a session, check the approved build, valid bounded invitation, remaining quota and backup. Revoke unused/reusable invitation access afterward. End and clear session deletes the RAM profile/feedback; daily usage and minimal logs have separate retention. A local cookie is not personal identity verification. Do not reset/delete the ledger to bypass caps.

| Measure                 | Record only after an approved exercise                             | Interpretation                             |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Task completion         | Success/total; assistance needed                                   | Local task completion, not market fit      |
| Source understanding    | Can identify evidence and synthetic/official distinction           | A source click alone is insufficient       |
| Constraints/uncertainty | Can explain hard limit, conditional permission and unknown funding | Comprehension, not admission eligibility   |
| Next action             | Concrete verification step in their words                          | Utility of the step, no guaranteed outcome |
| Time/stalls             | Approximate duration and redacted confusion point                  | Improve after actual measurement           |
| Reuse intent            | yes/no/unsure and nonpersonal reason                               | Intent, not retention/revenue              |

Master goal 5 bachelor + 5 master + 2 PhD, diverse fields and people beyond close contacts is a future target, not twelve completed tests. Real programme decisions require reviewed published data and approved processing/hosting. Use outside CS is unproven.

## Stop and follow up

Stop on suspected disclosure, external cost, fabricated fact, hard-rule bypass or enabled upload. End/revoke, record minimal incident facts and disclose why offline backup is used. Do not request private evidence. Separate UI bug, missing data, misunderstanding and unverified institutional claim; prioritise safety/critical facts.
