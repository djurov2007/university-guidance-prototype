# Demo limitations and risks

2026-10-11, `phase1-demo-1`. [Step 11 issues](../testing/KNOWN_ISSUES.md), [release checklist](RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md). Include these limits in every presentation/tester package.

- **Data:** published 1.0.3 has eight human-reviewed programmes, 31 official sources, 145 facts and 26 accepted visible unknown. Individual fee/qualification/funding bindings remain manual. Synthetic demo evidence/programmes stay fictional, with fixed fixture dates and no official URL; the master subjects rule belongs to no real institution.
- **AI:** explanations/selectors are mock/fake. General NLP, stochastic model quality, real-model injection resilience and live accounting remain unproven. The short supported budget phrase is not general conversation understanding. Paid AI is disabled.
- **Rules:** confirmed hard constraints, mandatory checks and scoped alternatives are tested for defined cases. Unknown is not satisfied; likely met is not official confirmation. Three cards is a maximum. A limited-catalogue no-match is not global absence.
- **Operation:** loopback/local_http on this computer; local SQLite control hashes and quota/use history, RAM profile/history/feedback, 30-minute session. Restart loses the profile; deletion doesn't erase day totals/logs. No distributed identity/persistence/HA/public TLS proof.
- **Privacy:** adult statement is not identity verification or legal pilot approval. Real documents/PII processing is unauthorised. Notice/24h minimal logs are a local baseline, not independent legal/security certification. SSD/backup erase and provider retention unverified.
- **Dependencies:** Step 11 audit dated 2026-10-11: five high dev ESLint/glob/braces findings, zero prod advisories. Findings remain open, no major downgrade or clean-toolchain claim. Local recorder compatibility tool is old and isolated to trusted UI frames, not arbitrary media ingestion.
- **Accessibility:** keyboard/labels/selected solid contrasts and 320/390/1280px states tested. Screen reader, forced colours, full zoom/device coverage and WCAG certification NOT RUN.
- **Evidence:** software/golden/browser checks are synthetic internal evidence. No real students, interviews, paying users, measured time saving, pilot results or market fit. The 50-case set is development data, not blind 100-case evaluation.
- **Presentation:** one-pager, seven-slide outline, script and backup are prepared materials, not messages or a claimed PPTX deck. MBZUAI/other institutions aren't confirmed partners. Roles/expertise/funding aren't invented.

Local catalogue publication is complete only for approved 1.0.3. Readiness doesn't open live/upload/cloud/pilot or Phase 2. Follow named-owner gates in RELEASE_CHECKLIST; disclose new critical issues instead of changing caps/evidence/results to hide them.
